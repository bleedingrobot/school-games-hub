# Quick looks pictures

There are no painted pictures in this folder yet. It holds `README.md` (the names and sizes James can drop in) and
`index.ts` (finds them by name). Until a picture exists the code draws a stand-in, so nothing here is third-party art.

The one painted picture for Quick looks is the home page card, `src/assets/home/quick-looks.webp` and
`quick-looks-small.webp`. It was painted in the clay style on James's gaming PC with ComfyUI by
`scripts/quick-looks-card-art.py`, which borrows Weka's graph and style words from `scripts/weka-art.py` (so that script
must stay). The card's prompt, a ladybird called Dot in a garden, is in the script. Repo history: card added 4 Nov 2026
(#200), repainted in clay 5 Nov 2026 (#229). The repo does not record the licence terms of the image model, so they are
not stated here.

# Class Showdown art

All pictures in `paint/` and the home card (`src/assets/home/showdown*.webp`) were painted for School Games Hub on James's own computer with ComfyUI, from the prompts in `docs/showdown-art/config.json` (picks in `docs/showdown-art/picks.json`), 23 Nov 2026. Original creatures and scenes; no third-party art.

The chest pictures (`paint/ui/chest-shut.webp`, `paint/ui/chest-open.webp`) are copies of Chess Quest's painted chests (`src/games/chess/art/fx/`), also painted for School Games Hub.

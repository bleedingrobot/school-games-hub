# Sky Isles pictures

Paintings made by James with Z-Image Turbo in ComfyUI, November 2026, in the hand-painted anime style he chose
(docs/ISLES-ART-PLAN.md). Which try of each is used: docs/isles-art/picks.json. Cut out, trimmed and resized by
`scripts/isles-art.py tidy`.

- **2 Nov 2026:** the island bases, buildings, sky-boats, Pip, the Sky Keepers and the three backgrounds.
- **4 Nov 2026 (cosy batch and stage S.7):** new Farm level 2 and 3 and Windmill level 1, the sky garden, 12
  decorations, 6 islanders, the coin, the sky crystal, 3 gifts, 4 weather cards, the Lighthouse's 4 stages, the Gull
  Riders' boat, 3 scenes (night, season's end, Hall of the Skies) and the home page card (`src/assets/home/isles*.webp`).

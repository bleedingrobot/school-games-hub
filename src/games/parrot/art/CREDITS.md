# Parrot's See-saw pictures

Everything in `../art.tsx` (Parrot and the friends, the see-saw, pans, stones, numbered weights, the heap, the shiny things on
the bell rope and the Golden Bell) is a stand-in drawn in code as SVG, by Claude, for the build of this game. Nothing here is a
third-party picture or a painted one. They stay until the painted art pass: the prompts and picks will be in
`docs/parrot-art/config.json` (to be added), and painted pictures will go in `paint/` here.

# Chess pictures

The painted pictures in this folder were made for School Games Hub with an image AI (ComfyUI on James's PC), from the
prompts in [docs/chess-art/config.json](../../../../docs/chess-art/config.json) and [fx.json](../../../../docs/chess-art/fx.json), and shrunk with `scripts/game_art.py`.
They were not copied from any game, artist or art pack. The pieces in `../pieces/` have their own NOTICE.md.

| Folder | What | Made | Tool |
|---|---|---|---|
| bg/ | Backgrounds behind the board: the castle hall everyone has, and the six to unlock (forest, coast, volcano, snowy mountain, underwater palace, space station) | 18 Nov 2026 | Image AI |
| fx/ | The treasure chest (shut and open) and the stand for the unlock reveal | 18 Nov 2026 | Image AI, cut out with rembg |

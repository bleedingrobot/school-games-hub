# Dragon Maths pictures

The painted pictures in this folder were made for this game by James (the teacher who runs School Games Hub) with an
image AI, from the prompts in [docs/lord-art/PROMPTS.md](../../../../docs/lord-art/PROMPTS.md), then cropped, shrunk and
cut out with `scripts/lord-art.py` (figures on painted backgrounds were cut out with the open-source rembg tool, run on the server only). They were not copied from any game, artist or art pack.

Every picture is from the darker painted set of 26 Oct 2026 (the first, brighter storybook set of 22 Oct is in git history).

| Folder | What | Made | Tool |
|---|---|---|---|
| scenes/ | Places and the kingdom map | 26 Oct 2026 (the inn redone the same day) | Image AI |
| people/ | The village people (innkeeper, blacksmith, healer, banker, bard) | 26 Oct 2026 | Image AI |
| heroes/ | 120 looks a pupil can pick (40 per class: 10 girls and 10 boys, each painted twice), each with a -small copy for the picker | 26 Oct 2026 | Image AI |
| masters/ | The eleven masters | 26 Oct 2026 (redone the same day) | Image AI |
| monsters/ | 120 forest monsters | 26 Oct 2026 | Image AI |
| dragon/ | Scorchtail | 26 Oct 2026 | Image AI |

Weapons, armour and icons are still Kenney's 1-Bit Pack (CC0, see ../KENNEY-LICENSE.txt).
Headings use IM Fell English SC by Igino Marini (SIL Open Font Licence 1.1, see ../IMFELL-OFL.txt).

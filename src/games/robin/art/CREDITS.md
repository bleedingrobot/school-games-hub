# Robin's Berry Basket pictures

Everything in `../art.tsx` (Robin and the friends, the berries, the basket and its numbered slots, the covered plate, the ten
baskets on the log and the Golden Berry Feast) is a stand-in drawn in code as SVG, by Claude, for the build of this game.
Nothing here is a third-party picture or a painted one. They stay until the painted art pass: the prompts and picks are in
`docs/robin-art/config.json`, and painted pictures will go in `paint/` here.

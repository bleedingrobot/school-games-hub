# Royal Decree pictures

The painted villagers in this folder were made for this game on 6 November 2026 with an image AI on James's own computer
(James is the teacher who runs School Games Hub), from the prompts in
[docs/decree-art/PROMPTS.md](../../../../docs/decree-art/PROMPTS.md), in the same bright hand-painted animated-film style as Kingdom Keeper. They were
then cut out, shrunk and given a -small copy with `scripts/decree-art.py` (figures were cut off their white paper with the open-source
rembg tool, run on James's computer only). They were not copied from any game, artist or art pack.

| Folder | What |
|---|---|
| people/ | The 26 new villagers who come to the throne, each with a -small copy (the first six villagers reuse Kingdom Keeper's pictures) |

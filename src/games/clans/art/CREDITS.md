# Clan Quest pictures

The painted pictures in this folder were made for this game by James (the teacher who runs School Games Hub) with the
Z-Image Turbo image model in ComfyUI on their own PC, in October 2026. Each picture's prompt and workflow are saved inside
the original PNG (kept in the private `bleedingrobot/clans-art-source` repo). They were then shrunk and cut out with
`scripts/clans-art.py` (figures were cut out with the open-source rembg tool, run on the server only). They were not
copied from any game, artist or art pack.

Every picture was checked for children aged 10–12 on 26 Oct 2026; seven were rejected and remade. Everything except the
members was painted twice, and `docs/clans-art/default-picks.json` records which copy is used.

| Folder | What | Made |
|---|---|---|
| scenes/ | The places, fight backgrounds (the five mine zones) and moments (battlefield, victory, rest), each with an -800 copy for phones | Oct 2026 |
| quests/ | One picture per quest, with -800 copies | Oct 2026 |
| kingdoms/ | The six kingdoms you can raid, with -800 copies | Oct 2026 |
| members/ | Four looks (two girls, two boys) for every race and class, each with a -small copy | Oct 2026 (the imp sorcerer and barbarian girls and the minotaur martial artist girl remade 26 Oct) |
| monsters/ | Every monster in the Mines (the Horse Archer was the Centaur) | Oct 2026 (the baby dragon remade 26 Oct) |
| items/ | Weapons, armour and shields | Oct 2026 |
| troops/, buildings/ | The empire's troops and buildings | Oct 2026 |
| banners/ | One banner per clan name (Owls, Bears and so on) | Oct 2026 |

Where a picture isn't there, the game still shows Kenney's 1-Bit Pack tiles (CC0, see ../KENNEY-LICENSE.txt).

# Maths Monsters pictures

The painted pictures in this folder were made for this game by James (the teacher who runs School Games Hub) with
Z-Image Turbo in ComfyUI, on his own computer, in October 2026 (see
[docs/prompts/maths-monsters-art.md](../../../../../docs/prompts/maths-monsters-art.md)). They were then cut out with the
open-source rembg tool, shrunk and cropped by `scripts/monsters-art.py`, run on this computer only, never in the site
build. They were not copied from any game, artist or art pack.

| Folder | What | Sizes |
|---|---|---|
| monsters/ | The 54 species, plain and shiny | 384 square, `-small` 128 |
| people/ | The 50 people, nurses and trainers on the maps | 640 tall, `-small` 200 head and shoulders |
| looks/ | 24 trainer looks a pupil can pick (`boy-01` … `girl-12`) | 640 tall, `-small` 200 head and shoulders |
| scenes/ | Battle backgrounds: the places by map id, and the six Topic Towers (`tower-1` … `tower-6`) | 1600 wide, `-800` |
| badges/ | The six badges | 256 square |
| items/ | The five Bag items | 256 square |

The map itself is still Kenney pixel art (CC0, see ../../world/assets/kenney).

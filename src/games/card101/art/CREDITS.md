# 101 painted art

All pictures in `paint/` were painted for this game by James Riley on his own computer with ComfyUI (local model), 28 Nov 2026,
from the prompts in `docs/101-art/config.json`, and tidied with `scripts/game_art.py 101 tidy`. Picks: `docs/101-art/picks.json`.
The Pāua back is cropped from inside a painted shell; the Classic and Matariki backs had a painted frame trimmed off.
Sea Glass, Rock Pool and the round-win sparkle use Bond Match's painted art (see `src/games/bond-match/art/CREDITS.md`).

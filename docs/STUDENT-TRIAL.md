# Student trial checklist (sign-in and bot games)

A short trial with a small group (4–6 students, about 20 minutes) before multiplayer is built.
Live site: https://bleedingrobot.github.io/school-games-hub/

## The day before, and again on the morning

- [ ] **Is the database awake?** The free Supabase project goes to sleep after a week with nobody using it. Open the live site and sign in. If sign-in fails or the dashboard says it can't load your games, log in at supabase.com, open **school-games-hub** and click **Restore project**. It takes a few minutes and nothing is lost.
- [ ] On a school Chromebook, sign in with your own school account.
- [ ] Play a quick checkers game against **Very Easy**, then resign. The result card should say **✓ Saved to your games**, and the game should appear under **Recent games** on the dashboard.
- [ ] Do the same with chess.
- [ ] Check the students know their school Google login, and that their Chromebooks are charged.
- [ ] Print one problem slip per student (bottom of this page).

## What students try

1. Sign in with **Sign in with Google**, using their school account.
2. Pick a player name and build an avatar.
3. Play **checkers against Very Easy**, as Red (you go first).
4. Play checkers again as **White**, against Easy or Medium.
5. Play **chess** against Very Easy or Easy.
6. In one checkers game, press **Resign**, then **Rematch**.
7. Go back to the dashboard and find their games under **Recent games** and **My stats**.
8. Sign out: tap their avatar (top right), then **Sign out** at the bottom of that page.

## What to watch for

- Anyone stuck at sign-in. Write down the exact words on the screen, e.g. *access_not_configured* (that means the Google admin needs to approve the app).
- Students who don't know how to move, or don't understand why a piece won't move when they *must jump*. Do they notice the glowing pieces and the yellow note?
- Squares or buttons that are hard to tap, or anything that doesn't fit on the screen.
- The Chromebook feeling slow while the Medium bot is thinking.
- Any **"couldn't save"** message.
- Bots that feel far too easy or far too hard.
- Any player name or avatar that seems unkind or out of place.

## How students report problems (no typing, nothing personal)

- The site has no chat and no typing, so problems are reported on **paper slips**, not in the app.
- Students tick boxes and draw a face. They don't write their real name. They can copy their **player name** from the screen (e.g. "Zippy Meerkat 79") if they like, so we can find the game.
- For anything odd, they put a hand up and show you. Note the **time** and the **player name**. With those, the saved game can be looked up later, and nothing personal is needed.

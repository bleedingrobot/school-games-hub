4. **Walking.** Arrow keys and WASD, smooth tile-to-tile steps, turning on the spot, blocked by trees, water and people. On touch screens: tap a tile to walk there by the shortest path, plus an optional on-screen D-pad (off by default, a setting). Space / Enter or tap to talk and read signs. The camera follows you and stops at the map edges. Walking is never faster than the student can follow.
5. **Encounters.** Walking in long grass starts a wild battle about once every 12-20 steps (put the numbers in one easy-to-edit block, and use a seeded random so tests are repeatable). The wild monster comes from the area, at a level in the area's range. A "Repel" idea is for later, but leave a place for it. Win, catch, run or lose all come back to the map: after a loss you wake up at the healing centre with everything kept; a caught monster joins the team (or the box if the team is full).
6. **Trainers and people.** A trainer who sees you in line (a few tiles ahead) walks up and challenges you, with a `trainer` battle at `far` = true. They only fight you once per visit to the area (this is device-only, see 8). People and signs show their text in an HTML box under the map that is read out by screen readers (live region), one tap to go on, never any typing or student-made text.
7. **Healing centre.** Walk in, talk to the nurse, the team is healed (`healTeam`), and this becomes the place you wake up after losing.
8. **Game state.** One plain object: where you are (map, tile, facing), team, box, bag (starter: 5 potions, 10 balls), which trainers you have beaten, and the last healing spot. For N.4 it lives only in memory on the test page (nothing saved anywhere; saving is N.6). Keep it in a shape that can be saved later without changes.

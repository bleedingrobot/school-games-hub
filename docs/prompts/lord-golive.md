## How to turn it off again
The teacher page's switch, or `update public.app_settings set lord_enabled = false where id;` (heroes are kept; pupils see "the village gates are closed"; the season clock pauses). To remove the function: delete `lord` in the Supabase dashboard. To take the points away after a bad day: the teacher's "new season" button clears Dragon Maths points only (heroes, stars, the Hall and achievements stay).

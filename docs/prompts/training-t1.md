Build T.1:

1. **Facts and families** (`shared/training/`): every times-table and division fact up to 12 × 12, grouped into fact families (6×7, 7×6, 42÷6, 42÷7), with each fact's step on the ladder. All numbers, bands and session sizes go in `shared/training/config.ts` so I can change them without touching code.
2. **Session builder:** about 3 minutes or 20–30 questions, mostly facts the student is learning now, some review of older facts (weakest and most overdue first), and a few known facts, finishing on a known one (plan section 3). Struggling students get the smaller "2 new among about 4 known" set. Same seed gives the same session, so a server can rebuild it later.
3. **Hidden timing:** time each answer on the device (`performance.now()`, from when the question is fully shown to when it's submitted). No clock, countdown or score is ever shown during a session.
4. **Typing check:** at the start of a session (and the first time ever) the student types two or three numbers they can see. Keep a typing time for 1-digit and 2-digit answers, using the middle of the last several checks. **Thinking time = answer time − typing time** for that answer's length, never below zero.
5. **Speed bands** exactly as in plan section 5: under 1.5 s bright green "Just know it!", 1.5–3.5 light green "Quick thinking", 3.5–5 yellow "Getting there", 5–8 light orange "Working it out", 8–10 soft blue "Still learning", 10 s or more grey-blue "New to me". No red anywhere. Students see colour and label only, never seconds. A fact's colour is the middle of its last 3 answers, and a wrong answer counts as "New to me". "Known" means bright green on two different days; light green is enough to keep moving on.
6. **Fix-it moment** (plan 4.6): after a wrong answer, show the right answer with an array picture of the fact, the student types it in, and it doesn't count as right.
7. **End-of-session summary:** how many facts they practised and which ones changed colour ("3 facts turned green!"). Never "you got slower".
8. **My facts map** (plan section 6): the 12×12 grid in speed colours, with 6×7 and 7×6 colouring together and division showing on the same squares. Facts due for review glow softly and never lose their colour. Tapping a square shows its fact family.
9. **Number pad:** big buttons, keyboard and number keys work, and an optional auto-submit once the right number of digits is typed.
10. **Preview** in `preview/training`, with records saved in the browser only (wrapped so it still works if storage is blocked). Add test buttons: reset, "pretend it's tomorrow" (so I can check two-day "known" and review), and fill the map with sample records so I can see every colour.

Not in T.1: strategy lessons, early facts, Quick-fire 20, the too-fast lock, mistake patterns, fractions, the garden, teacher screens, the database and a hub page. Those are later stages in plan section 13.

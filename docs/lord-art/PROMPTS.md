# Dragon Maths – picture prompts

These are the prompts for the new Dragon Maths pictures. Paste each one into an image AI (ChatGPT, Gemini or similar),
then paste the picture back into the Claude chat **with its number** (for example "P3"), or save it as
`~/dragon-art-inbox/P3.png` on the server. Claude trims, shrinks and wires them in. Until a picture arrives, the game
keeps its old one, so nothing breaks while you go.

**Tips**

- Do them all in **one chat** with the image AI, and start with P1. For later pictures say "same style as the
  earlier pictures" (it's already in each prompt). That keeps the look consistent.
- If a picture has words or letters in it, or anything too scary, ask it to "make it again without the writing" or
  "make it friendlier".
- Ask for the shape given (**landscape**, **portrait** or **square**). Claude crops to fit, so exact sizes don't matter.
- Monster sheets: the AI may mix up the order or draw a few too many. That's fine; Claude matches each monster by
  eye. If one is clearly wrong, Claude will ask for just that one again.
- The style references were only a guide. Nothing here names a game or an artist, and nothing should be copied
  from one.

**Style line** (already at the start of every prompt below):

> Painted storybook fantasy art, warm light, soft mist, rich deep colours, detailed but clear, friendly for children
> aged 8 to 11, nothing gory, no writing, letters or numbers anywhere in the picture.

The story: the village of **Hollowbrook** sits beside a big forest. A red dragon, **Scorchtail**, stole the village's
lanterns and treasure and sleeps on them in a cave in the mountains. Heroes train with masters, fight forest monsters
and finally face the dragon.

## Scoring: each criterion 1–4
1 = missing or harmful, 2 = present but weak, 3 = good, 4 = excellent / matches research best practice.
1. Maths fidelity: teaches the idea correctly; winning needs the maths (not guessing or tapping everything).
2. Clarity: a 5–7-year-old can tell what to do without an adult (pre-readers, read-aloud, one thing at a time).
3. Support and structure: hints, models (ten-frames, number lines, part-whole) available when stuck.
4. Fading: support steps back as the child succeeds, and comes back if they struggle.
5. Progression: difficulty builds in research order, and adapts to the child.
6. Feedback: after a mistake it shows what went wrong and why, not just right/wrong.
7. Progress recording: what is saved per child on the server; could a teacher see who is stuck on what?
8. Engagement: fun because of the maths, or despite it.

Evidence: file:line references, and screenshots where you played it. Don't change any game code.

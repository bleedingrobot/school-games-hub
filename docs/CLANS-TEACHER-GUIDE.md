# Clan Quest: a short guide for the first class trial

For the teacher. About two minutes to read.

## What it is
Each pupil makes a clan of four heroes, goes down the Mines to fight monsters (with a maths question before each round that can help), spends gold in the market, trains their heroes, visits the Church, does one short quest a day and builds a small empire. Each pupil has their own clan, kept on the school's server, so it is the same on any Chromebook. There is no chat. The clan name is always picked from lists, and so are hero names unless you switch on **made-up hero names**.

**Made-up hero names** (off until you switch them on, in the Clan Quest panel): pupils making a new clan can type their own name for any hero instead of picking from the list. The list is still the starting choice. Names are letters only (one space, dash or apostrophe allowed), up to 12 long, and the server refuses rude words. Only the pupil and you see hero names; classmates never do. On each pupil's page you can see their heroes' names and, if one slips through, press **Swap made-up names for list names**. Switching it off later keeps names already made. Remind pupils not to use their own or a classmate's real name.

## Switching it on
1. Open the **Teacher view**. Find the **Clan Quest** panel.
2. **Switch Clan Quest on.** The card appears on every pupil's home page. **Switch it off** and the card disappears and nothing can be played. Clans are kept while it is off.
3. You can always open Clan Quest yourself (from the address `#/clan-quest`), even when it is off, to try it first. Your clan is a real clan on your account.

## How to play, for the pupils
Pupils have a **How to play** button on their clan page, and a "How to play" box on the very first screen where they make their clan. It explains the Mines, the Battle Cry, the shop, training, the Church, the empire, quests, the village, raids (and that you can switch them off), fame, the scoreboard and achievements, in short sections. Its numbers come from the game's own settings, including the mine fights a day you choose, so it never goes out of date.

## For pupils who need a different way to play
- Everything can be done with the **keyboard** alone: Tab and Enter move around, and in a fight each round starts with a suggested plan for the whole clan: **Enter** fights with it and the number keys change one hero's order first (then **A** attack, **S** spell, **G** guard, **R** run, the number keys pick from a list and **Esc** goes back to the plan). The focus ring is a thick yellow outline.
- Every button is at least 44 pixels tall, so it works on a touch screen. The text and borders have strong colour contrast on the dark background, and nothing is shown by colour alone (health bars have their numbers written on them, tags say "Up next" or "resting" in words).
- If a pupil's computer is set to **reduce motion**, the health bars and the achievement sparkle stop moving. The page also works at 200% zoom with nothing cut off (it scrolls).
- Screen readers hear each screen's heading when it opens, the fight's orders and Battle Cry question have names, and the fight log is announced as it grows.

## A day in the game
- Each clan gets **8 mine fights a day** (you can change that, see below), one quest, one raid on a computer kingdom, and one free wake-up at the Church.
- Everything comes back **at midnight, New Zealand time**. A clan nobody opened still gets its night's rest, and never twice.
- A fight can be left half-finished: closing the page or opening another Chromebook picks it up where it was. It does not give a free retry and it does not use another fight.
- A wrong maths answer never blocks anything. It just means the help (extra damage or healing) is not given, and the working is shown.
- The questions are made and marked by the game, never by an AI. You choose the year band and the topics on the Teacher view (see **The maths** below).

## The class village
- Each pupil's clan belongs to a **village**, a group that builds together. Until you make groups, every pupil is in one village called **Everyone else**. In the **Clan Quest villages** panel on the Teacher view you can **make a group** (for example "Room 12"), **move pupils** into it with the drop-down beside each name, rename a group, or delete one (its pupils go back to "Everyone else" and its chest is lost).
- Pupils can **give gold to the village chest** from their clan purse. Nobody has to give, and nobody can take gold out. Gold they give stays with the group they were in when they gave it.
- Each **week** (Monday to Sunday, New Zealand time) the group **votes** on one building: Training Hall (cheaper training), Smithy (a better shop) or Church (one more free wake-up a day for each level). One vote each, picked from buttons; a pupil can change their vote until the week ends. At Monday's midnight the winner is built if the chest has enough gold (a tie goes to the cheaper building). If the chest is short, nothing is built and the gold stays.
- The **Village** screen shows the class goal as a progress bar ("Smithy level 1: 120 / 250 gold") with a small maths question about it. A wrong answer blocks nothing.
- The **Village news** page shows lines the game writes: fights won, bosses beaten, buildings built, raids. They name **clans only** (for example "A raider from the Red Banner clan took 40 gold."), never a pupil. You can see which pupil has which clan on their page.

## Raids on classmates
- **Off until you switch them on** (the Raids switch in the Clan Quest villages panel). While off, the screen says so and the server refuses every raid.
- Pupils can only raid someone **in their own group**, and only a clan whose army is about the same size (never more than twice as big or small). The rules that keep it fair: a new clan is safe for 3 days, one raid on a classmate a day, never the same clan twice in a day, at most 10% of the loot, and a clan that was just raided is shielded for a day.
- A raid can take some of the other clan's **vault gold**, some of their **empty land**, or knock down some **buildings** (walls protect them). It never touches the clan purse or the village chest. The raided clan reads what happened next time they open the game ("While you were away"), and the village news says which clan did it.

## The maths
In the **Clan Quest maths** panel on the Teacher view you choose, for the whole school:
- the **year band** (4, 5 or 6), which sets how hard the numbers are;
- which **topics** are asked: times tables, percentages, money and budgets, area and perimeter, division and remainders, ratios, better buys, negative numbers (at least one stays on). When times tables are on you also pick which tables;
- how many **mine fights a day** each clan gets (1 to 30; 8 until you change it).

Press **Save settings**. Pupils get the new settings next time they open Clan Quest. A fight that is already open keeps the questions it started with, so a change never alters a fight in progress. There is no Practice Mode: nothing in Clan Quest is a test.

Every Battle Cry answer and every quest puzzle answer (right or wrong) is kept in a private record that only you can see. The panel shows which kinds of question the school finds hardest, and each pupil's page shows how they are getting on, hardest first. The records only say the *kind* of question and the year band (for example "Percent of (Year 5)"), never a pupil's numbers. The game cannot tell how long an answer took, so none of this is a speed score.

## Fame, points and achievements
- **Fame** is what the **Scoreboard** inside the game ranks clans by. A mine win is 1 fame, a boss 5, a Friendly War win 2, a raid won on a computer kingdom 2 and on a classmate 3, and when the class builds a village building everyone who voted that week gets 10. Fame only goes up, and **never comes from maths scores**. The Scoreboard shows the top 10 clans in the whole school and the pupil's own clan, with **clan names and fame only** (never a pupil's name).
- **New season:** in the **Clan Quest fame season** panel, **Start a new season…** (you type NEW SEASON to confirm) sets every clan's fame back to 0 and keeps everything else (clans, gold, levels, empires, achievements, points). The school-wide *new points season* further up the page only clears Clan Quest's points, not fame.
- **Points** (inside the usual 40-a-day limit that games against the computer share): 1 point for every 3 mine fights won, 3 for a boss, 2 for a Friendly War win, and 5 for everyone who voted in a week when the class built something. Raids earn fame but no points. Nothing is paid twice, however many times a request is sent.
- **Achievements** (eight, worth 10 points each like every game's): first win, boss beaten, level 10, level 20, five buildings, a Friendly War win, helping the village build something, and 50 Battle Cry questions answered right. The pupil sees a small celebration, and the clan page lists them all.
- Clan Quest is **not ranked** on the hub's leaderboards.

## If something goes wrong
- **A pupil is stuck, or wants to start again:** Teacher view → pick the pupil → **Clan Quest** → **Reset this clan…**. Their clan is deleted and they make a new one. Points and achievements stay (fame goes with the clan).
- **"Clan Quest isn't switched on yet":** the switch is off.
- **"Your clan was busy":** they pressed two things at once; try again.
- **A fight shows "in the middle of a fight":** the pupil can finish it. Nothing else about their clan can change until it is over.

# Clan Quest: a short guide for the first class trial

For the teacher. About two minutes to read.

## What it is
Each pupil makes a clan of four heroes, goes down the Mines to fight monsters (with a maths question before each round that can help), spends gold in the market, trains their heroes, visits the Church, does one short quest a day and builds a small empire. Each pupil has their own clan, kept on the school's server, so it is the same on any Chromebook. There is no chat and no free text: the clan name and hero names are picked from lists.

## Switching it on
1. Open the **Teacher view**. Find the **Clan Quest** panel.
2. **Switch Clan Quest on.** The card appears on every pupil's home page. **Switch it off** and the card disappears and nothing can be played. Clans are kept while it is off.
3. You can always open Clan Quest yourself (from the address `#/clan-quest`), even when it is off, to try it first. Your clan is a real clan on your account.

## A day in the game
- Each clan gets **8 mine fights a day**, one quest, one raid on a computer kingdom, and one free wake-up at the Church.
- Everything comes back **at midnight, New Zealand time**. A clan nobody opened still gets its night's rest, and never twice.
- A fight can be left half-finished: closing the page or opening another Chromebook picks it up where it was. It does not give a free retry and it does not use another fight.
- A wrong maths answer never blocks anything. It just means the help (extra damage or healing) is not given, and the working is shown.
- The questions are made and marked by the game, never by an AI. The topics follow the usual defaults (Year 5) for now; the Teacher page for maths topics comes with the next stage.

## The class village
- Each pupil's clan belongs to a **village**, a group that builds together. Until you make groups, every pupil is in one village called **Everyone else**. In the **Clan Quest villages** panel on the Teacher view you can **make a group** (for example "Room 12"), **move pupils** into it with the drop-down beside each name, rename a group, or delete one (its pupils go back to "Everyone else" and its chest is lost).
- Pupils can **give gold to the village chest** from their clan purse. Nobody has to give, and nobody can take gold out. Gold they give stays with the group they were in when they gave it.
- Each **week** (Monday to Sunday, New Zealand time) the group **votes** on one building: Training Hall (cheaper training), Smithy (a better shop) or Church (one more free wake-up a day for each level). One vote each, picked from buttons; a pupil can change their vote until the week ends. At Monday's midnight the winner is built if the chest has enough gold (a tie goes to the cheaper building). If the chest is short, nothing is built and the gold stays.
- The **Village** screen shows the class goal as a progress bar ("Smithy level 1: 120 / 250 gold") with a small maths question about it. A wrong answer blocks nothing.
- The **Village news** page shows lines the game writes: fights won, bosses beaten, buildings built, raids. They name **clans only** (for example "A raider from the Red Banner clan took 40 gold."), never a pupil. You can see which pupil has which clan on their page.

## Raids on classmates
- **Off until you switch them on** (the Raids switch in the Clan Quest villages panel). While off, the screen says so and the server refuses every raid.
- Pupils can only raid someone **in their own group**, and only a clan whose army is about the same size (never more than twice as big or small). The rules that keep it fair: a new clan is safe for 3 days, one raid on a classmate a day, never the same clan twice in a day, at most 10% of the loot, and a clan that was just raided is shielded for a day.
- A raid can take some of the other clan's **vault gold**, some of their **empty land**, or knock down some **buildings** (walls protect them). It never touches the clan purse or the village chest. The raided clan reads what happened next time they open the game ("While you were away"), and the village news says which clan did it.

## If something goes wrong
- **A pupil is stuck, or wants to start again:** Teacher view → pick the pupil → **Clan Quest** → **Reset this clan…**. Their clan is deleted and they make a new one. Points and achievements stay.
- **"Clan Quest isn't switched on yet":** the switch is off.
- **"Your clan was busy":** they pressed two things at once; try again.
- **A fight shows "in the middle of a fight":** the pupil can finish it. Nothing else about their clan can change until it is over.

## What is not in yet
Scoreboard, points and achievements, and the maths topics page. These come in the next stage. Until then Clan Quest gives **no points** and does not appear on leaderboards.

# Profile review: the pupils' "My profile" page and rewards

*Review only, 12 November 2026, from `main` at e160759f, the home preview at Chromebook size (1366×768, made-up pupil) and read-only counts from the live database. No code was changed. The step-by-step plan is in [plans/profile/PLAN.md](plans/profile/PLAN.md), with copy-ready prompts on the "Profile and Trophies Plan" artifact page (link in [plans/profile/HANDOVER.md](plans/profile/HANDOVER.md)).*

**One goal:** a child opens their profile and sees who they are and what they have won, and the best rewards are worth coming back for, season after season.

## Summary for James

- **The page is more than four screens long and the avatar is not on the first one.** 3,219 px tall on a Chromebook with only 6 achievements shown (the real hub has 196), with 71 things to tap. Sign out is the very last thing on the page.
- **Levels have stopped meaning anything.** 100 of 162 pupils (62%) are level 1 and only 1 has reached level 7. A level unlocks nothing, classmates never see it, the 12 early-years games give no points at all, and levels are wiped at the yearly New Season. My advice: retire the level number and bar, keep points quietly for "points this week", and let trophies do the job.
- **Achievements are a long list, not a reward.** 196 kinds across 27 games, all shown at once, each just an emoji and a name. 115 pupils have at least one, the most is 49. Nothing looks special and classmates never see them.
- **There is no way to show off.** Every avatar is built from the same parts. Nothing a pupil does in a game changes how they look in the top bar, the leaderboard or the lobby.
- **Most records are wiped once a year.** Only Dragon Maths, Clan Quest, Maths Monsters and a few halls of fame last. Trophies need their own table that the New Season never touches.
- **Only Dragon Maths has real seasons** (35 days). Season 1 ends about 21 November and season 2 starts about 22 November. Five pupils have slain the dragon so far, so season 1 already has exactly its "first five".
- **Typed names are a promise change.** The privacy summary says the hub has no free typing. A custom name is possible, but it needs a teacher's Yes before anyone sees it, a rude-word check, and one new line in the privacy summary.
- **Missing today:** a teacher cannot reset a pupil's avatar, and 55 of 162 pupils have never made one.

**The plan, in order:** P1 tidy the page (sign out at the top), P2 plan and paint the art, P3 the trophy system, P4 the Dragon Maths dragon heads (before 22 November), P5 the trophy cabinet and earned pictures, P6 retire levels and show trophies around the hub, P7 and P8 trophies for every older game, P9 the early-years games, P10 rewards for coming back, two-game rewards and a school hall of fame, P11 earning your own name, P12 teacher tools, the yearly rollover and a last check as a child.

## 2. What's wrong, in order of how much it matters

1. **Sign out is last.** On shared Chromebooks this is the button every child needs at the end of every lesson.
2. **Too much at once.** Stats tables, every achievement in the hub and an 8-part avatar maker are all open together.
3. **The page leads with numbers, not the child.** Points and a level bar come first; the avatar and name come late.
4. **Achievements don't feel won.** Earned and not-yet-earned look much the same, and there are far too many to read.
5. **Nothing is rare.** Every avatar part is free from day one, so no picture says "I did something hard".
6. **Levels are dead weight** (see section 3).
7. **Names can be changed any number of times** with no record, which will matter once names can be earned.

## 3. Levels: my opinion

Retire them.

- 62% of pupils are level 1, 15% level 2, and one pupil is level 7. The ladder is too steep to feel.
- A level opens nothing and is shown in three places only the pupil sees.
- Younger pupils can never level up: the early-years games, Star Trader and Marble Rush give no points.
- The daily cap of 40 points means levels mostly measure how many days you turned up, which a trophy can say more clearly.
- They vanish at the yearly New Season.

Keep points themselves (they still drive "points this week" and stop any one game being farmed). Replace the level bar with a trophy count and the pupil's three favourite trophies.

## 4. The reward system I recommend

**Trophies.** A trophy is won for one hard feat in one game. It goes in the pupil's trophy cabinet for good. It is given by the server at the moment of the feat, and the yearly New Season never removes it.

**Bronze, silver, gold.** Most trophies can be won once per season. The first season is bronze, the second season is silver, the third is gold, and after that the gold one counts up (×4, ×5). The seasons don't need to be in a row. This is what rewards coming back.

**One season for the whole hub, on its own clock.** The hub gets its own season clock that James sets (start date and length, 35 days to begin with, and it can be paused). Every game's trophies use it, so "season 2" means the same weeks everywhere. It is not tied to Dragon Maths: pausing or changing the dragon's season does not move trophy seasons. The one exception is the Dragon Maths trophies, which follow the dragon's own season because that is the race they reward. (James, 12 Nov 2026.)

**Earned profile pictures.** The very hardest feat in each game also unlocks a painted picture the pupil can wear in place of their built avatar, with a bronze, silver or gold ring. It shows everywhere the avatar shows today (top bar, leaderboard, lobby, across the board from an opponent): about 30 places from one change.

**Two-game rewards.** Some of the best pictures need a feat in two different games (for example slaying the dragon and beating the clan monster in one season), and the Name Scroll needs gold in two different games. This pushes pupils to vary their play.

**Earn your name.** The top reward: a Name Scroll lets a pupil type their own player name. It goes to their teacher first. Nobody sees it until the teacher says Yes.

**Rules that keep it fair and safe.**

- A trophy shows a game feat, never a maths level. Nothing here ranks pupils by maths or shows a maths result to classmates.
- Early-years trophies come from days played and prizes finished. No races, no speed, no "first".
- A race trophy (first five) always sits beside a trophy anyone can win (slay the dragon at all), so slower starters still have something to aim for.
- Trophies give no hub points.
- The server checks a pupil really owns a picture before saving it.

## 6. Earning your own name: how it stays safe

- The pupil types 2 to 14 letters (one space allowed, no numbers or symbols). The server runs the Clan Quest rude-word check.
- The name waits in the teacher's "Needs you" list (the same place as Class Warp requests). The old name stays until the teacher says Yes.
- The teacher sees the old name and the new one, and can say No with one tap. A teacher can put the made-up name back at any time.
- One name per scroll. A list of past names is kept for teachers.
- The teacher is asked to say No to real names. The privacy summary gets one new line: "A pupil who earns it may type a player name, shown only after their teacher approves it."
- On for every class from the start; nothing is ever shown without a teacher's Yes.

## 7. Things you didn't ask for that I'd add

- **A "New trophy!" moment.** A full-screen celebration the next time the pupil is on a hub page, once per trophy.
- **Empty spots that tease.** The cabinet shows the next three trophies the pupil is closest to as grey shapes with "how to win it".
- **A school hall of fame.** A tab on the Leaderboard page: this season's first five dragon slayers, and every past season, kept for good.
- **New trophies on the teacher's Today page**, so you can give a shout-out in class (made-up names only, so it is safe on the projector).
- **Teacher tools:** see a pupil's trophies, reset an avatar to the plain one, give a Name Scroll, take a name back.
- **Back-award season 1.** The five pupils who have already slain the dragon get the first bronze Dragon Heads on the day this goes live.
- **A favourite three.** The pupil picks up to three trophies to show beside their name on the leaderboard and lobby.
- **Tidy-ups found on the way:** the Maths Monsters "full book" achievement still says 54 monsters (there are 75); pupils with no avatar get a nudge on the new profile page.

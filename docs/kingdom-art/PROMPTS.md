# Kingdom Keeper pictures: prompts

Painted on 26–27 October 2026 on James's ComfyUI (Z-Image Turbo), reusing the graph saved in a Dragon Maths gritty picture so the
model, sizes and steps match Dragon Maths exactly. Script: `~/dragon-art-inbox/tools/kingdom-art.py` (kept outside the repo, with the raw
PNGs in `~/dragon-art-inbox/kingdom-art/`). The chosen try of each picture is in [picks.json](picks.json); `scripts/kingdom-art.py all
~/dragon-art-inbox/kingdom-art` turns them into the game's WebP files.

Clothes are described in positive words only ("buttoned right up to the top button at the throat"): the model draws whatever a prompt names.

## The style sentence (every prompt starts with it, the same as Dragon Maths)

> Gritty dark fantasy digital painting, painterly concept art with visible brushstrokes, realistic proportions, weathered worn textures, grime and scratches, muted earthy palette with deep shadows and dramatic warm light, moody atmosphere, highly detailed. Suitable for children aged 8 to 11: no blood, no gore, not horror. No writing, letters or numbers anywhere.

People and rulers end with:

> Three-quarter length, from the knees up. Isolated on a plain flat pure white background, no shadow, no floor, no paint splatter, nothing else.

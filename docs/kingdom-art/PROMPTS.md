# Kingdom Keeper pictures: prompts

Painted on 27 October 2026 on James's ComfyUI (Z-Image Turbo, the same model and sizes as Dragon Maths), in the **animated-film** look James chose
from five try-outs. Script: `~/dragon-art-inbox/tools/kingdom-anim.py` (kept outside the repo, with the raw PNGs in `~/dragon-art-inbox/kingdom-anim/`).
One try per picture, repainted only when it failed a check. The chosen try of each is in [picks.json](picks.json); `scripts/kingdom-art.py all
~/dragon-art-inbox/kingdom-anim` turns them into the game's WebP files. The earlier gritty set's prompts are in git history.

Clothes, and empty places, are described in positive words only ("buttoned right up to the top button", "every seat empty"): the model draws whatever a prompt names.

## Style sentences

Outdoor scenes:

> Lush hand-painted background art from a cosy animated family film, gouache and watercolour, vivid summer greens and sky blues, towering white cumulus clouds, soft dappled sunlight, gentle nostalgic mood, highly detailed. Suitable for children aged 8 to 11. No writing, letters or numbers anywhere. 

Indoor scenes:

> Lush hand-painted background art from a cosy animated family film, gouache and watercolour, warm glowing colours, soft sunlight through the windows, gentle nostalgic mood, highly detailed. Suitable for children aged 8 to 11. No writing, letters or numbers anywhere. 

People and rulers:

> Character design for a cosy animated family film, clean simple cel-shaded shapes with soft outlines, hand-painted gouache texture, warm friendly expression. Suitable for children aged 8 to 11. No writing, letters or numbers anywhere. 

Places that must be empty end with:

> It is early morning before anyone has arrived: the place is quiet and deserted, every seat empty, only the objects described.

People and rulers end with:

> Full figure, standing. On a plain flat pure white background, nothing else.

Repaints: six rulers (the first tries showed a grown-up king beside a child; the ruler sentence now asks for one single child) and the map (clearer second try).

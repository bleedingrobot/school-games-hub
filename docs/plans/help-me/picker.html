What the review found

  • The ladder has 136 skills from Year 1 to Year 8, made of about 200 question types. 110 are on the auto ladder; 26 sit beside it and are asked by the junior games (Pipi, Hop, Penguin Pairs, Tūī, Pigeon, Parrot, Lizard, Swamp Hen, Robin), which draw and mark their own questions.
  • Only 8 skills have a free Help me box today: kinds of angles, remainders, x- and y-axis, adding fractions with different bottoms, hours to minutes (two skills), and the signs box on true-or-false and inequality questions.
  • Some games also have a Hint button that greys out two choices or says "between 50 and 60". Hints don't teach a method and right answers after one still count, so the rules below fold it into Help me.
  • Each times table holds only its new facts (×7 is 7 × 7, 7 × 8, 7 × 9…), so every newer table has easier facts below it. That makes the "use a fact you know" help a good fit for all the tables.
  • Answers are all saved by one shared step on the server, so "doesn't count after Help me" can be done once there rather than in every game.
  • Pictures: 12 ladder skills show a picture in the question today (perimeter, kinds of angles, clock, area, angles, coordinates, spinner, formulas, missing angles, polygons, compass degrees, circles), and the 26 junior-game skills are drawn by their games. About 47 more could use one: fraction bars, ten-frames, number lines through zero, the compass rose, clocks for time across the hour, thermometers. Each card has its own Yes / No / Maybe for the picture, and the "Could use a picture" filter lists them.
  • The 20 Year 4 skills you picked earlier aren't built yet. They'd get their help from this plan as they're built.

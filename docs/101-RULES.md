# How to play 101

Agreed with James on 16 October 2026 (swapped-in cards go face up and are locked: added the same day). The game's **How to play** panel is made from this file, and the rules code follows it exactly.

## The aim

Get the **lowest** hand each round. Your hand totals add up from round to round. When anyone's total reaches **101 or more**, the game ends, and the player with the **lowest total wins**.

- 2 to 4 players, one normal 52-card pack (no jokers).
- **Quick game:** the same rules, but the game ends at **50 or more** instead of 101.

## Card values

| Card | Points |
|---|---|
| King | 0 |
| 3 | 0, and it cancels your highest card (see below) |
| Ace | 1 |
| 2, 4, 5, 6, 7, 8, 9, 10 | The number on the card |
| Jack, Queen | 10 |

## Setting up each round

1. Everyone gets 4 cards, face down, in a 2×2 square.
2. **Peek:** look at your **bottom two** cards (the two nearest you). You only get to see them once, so remember them!
3. The rest of the pack goes face down in the middle: that's the **stack**. Its top card is turned face up next to it to start the **discard pile**.
4. The first round's starting player is picked at random. Each round after that, the start moves **one player along** round the table.

## Your turn

Do **one** of these:

**Take a card**
1. Take the top card of the **discard pile** (face up) or the top card of the **stack** (face down, only you see it).
2. Then either:
   - **Keep it:** swap it for one of your **face-down** cards. Your new card goes **face up**, so everyone can see it, and it stays there for the rest of the round: a face-up card can't be swapped again. The card you swapped out goes face up on the discard pile.
   - Once all four of your cards are face up, you can only throw away (or knock).
   - **Throw it away:** put it face up on the discard pile. (You can do this even with the card you just took from the discard pile.)

**Or knock**
- Instead of taking a card, knock to say "I think I'm lowest, let's finish this round."
- Nobody can knock until **everyone has had at least one turn** in the round.

Then play passes to the next player.

## If the stack runs out

Keep the top card of the discard pile where it is. Shuffle the rest of the discard pile face down to make a new stack, and carry on.

## Ending a round

After someone knocks, **every other player gets one last turn**. Then everyone turns their cards face up and scores their hand.

## Scoring your hand

Work it out in this order:

1. **Three of a kind** (three or four cards of the same rank): your **whole hand scores 0**. You're done!
2. **Pairs:** two cards of the same rank, anywhere in your square, cancel each other out. Both score 0. (Four of a kind is two pairs, so that's 0 too.)
3. **3s:** each 3 scores 0 **and** cancels your **highest card that's still counting**. If two cards tie for highest, the 3 cancels just one of them. Two 3s cancel two cards.
4. **Add up** what's left. That's your hand.

**Examples**

| Hand | Working | Score |
|---|---|---|
| 7, 7, 4, K | The 7s cancel. 4 + 0 | **4** |
| 5, 5, 5, Q | Three 5s: whole hand is 0 | **0** |
| 3, Q, 6, A | The 3 cancels the Queen. 6 + 1 | **7** |
| 3, 9, 9, 5 | The 9s cancel first. Then the 3 cancels the 5 | **0** |
| 3, 3, 9, 7 | One 3 cancels the 9, the other cancels the 7 | **0** |
| 3, J, Q, 2 | J and Q tie for highest (10). The 3 cancels one of them. 10 + 2 | **12** |

## Winning

- The lowest hand wins the round. There's no penalty for knocking if someone else ends up lower. If two players tie for the lowest hand, they both win the round. (It makes no difference to the scores, because everyone just adds their own hand.)
- Add your hand to your total. Shuffle, deal and play another round.
- When anyone's total reaches **101 or more** (50 in a Quick game) at the end of a round, the game ends. **Lowest total wins.** If two or more players tie for the lowest total, they **share the win**.

## Playing on the website

- **Against classmates:** if you don't take your turn within 60 seconds, your turn is skipped automatically. Get skipped 3 times in a row, or press **Leave**, and you're out of the game. It counts as a loss and earns no points. The others keep playing as long as at least 2 are left.
- **The peek** lasts 15 seconds, or until you press **I've remembered them**. After that, nobody can show you those cards again, not even by reloading the page.
